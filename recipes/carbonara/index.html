Carbonara

Ingredients

Per person:

  • 50 g aged guanciale
  • 80 g rigatoni
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Ground black pepper, to taste
  • 12.5 g pecorino romano, grated
  • Parmigiano reggiano for serving, to taste

Instructions

  1. Remove the rind from the guanciale, then cut it into cubes.
  2. Cook the guanciale in a dry pan over low heat to slowly render the fat. Spoon out the excess as it collects, reserving a little for the cream.
  3. Bring the pasta water to a boil and add 10 g of salt per liter. Cook the rigatoni until done.
  4. Meanwhile, mix the egg yolk with the black pepper and reserved guanciale fat to make a cream.
  5. Lift the guanciale out of the pan and drain it on two paper towels. Add the cooked pasta to the same pan with a splash of pasta water and let the excess liquid cook off.
  6. Take the pan off the heat and toss the pasta with the cream until evenly coated.
  7. Top with the crispy guanciale and serve with extra black pepper and grated parmigiano reggiano.
