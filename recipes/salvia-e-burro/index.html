Salvia e burro

Ingredients

Per person:

  • 1 egg
  • Semolina flour, enough to bring the dough together
  • 00 flour, for kneading
  • 50 g salted butter
  • 8 sage leaves
  • Grated parmigiano reggiano, to serve
  • Ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Mix the egg and teaspoon of water, then add semolina a little at a time until the dough comes together. Let it rest for 1 hour.
  2. Knead the dough with 00 flour, then roll it out and cut it into thick ribbons.
  3. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the pasta until done, then drain it.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the salted butter and sage in a pan over low heat until the sage leaves are crisp.
  5. Lift the sage out of the pan, then add the cooked pasta and toss it in the butter.
  6. Serve topped with the crispy sage, grated parmigiano reggiano, and black pepper to taste.
