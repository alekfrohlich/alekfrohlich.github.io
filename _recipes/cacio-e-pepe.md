## Ingredients

Per person:

- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon water
- Semolina flour, enough to bring the dough together
- 00 flour, for kneading
- 1 g ground black pepper, plus more to serve
- 50 g Pecorino Romano
- Extra grated cheese, to serve

## Instructions

1. Mix the egg and teaspoon of water, then add semolina a little at a time until the dough comes together. Let it rest for 1 hour.
2. Knead the dough with 00 flour, then roll it out and cut it into spaghetti.
3. Bring about half as much water as you would normally use for pasta to a boil. Cook the spaghetti until it is about 2 minutes shy of done.
4. Meanwhile, combine the ground black pepper with a little pasta water in a nonstick pan over low heat. Transfer the spaghetti to the pan and finish cooking it in the pepper mixture.
5. Mix the Pecorino Romano with a little pasta water to make a thick cream. Toss it with the spaghetti until the cheese is incorporated and the sauce has reduced enough to coat the pasta.
6. Serve with extra black pepper and grated cheese to taste.
